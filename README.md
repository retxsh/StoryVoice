# StoryVoice

StoryVoice is an exploration of voice-first manuscript drafting for fiction writers. Exploring how writers can turn spoken ideas, rough narration, and recorded thoughts into organised, editable manuscript material without losing their intended meaning, authorial voice, or creative control. The product focuses on faithful transcription, controlled AI transformation, and writer-approved story context, keeping the writer in control of every meaningful change.

## What This Covers

The work follows the complete product development lifecycle, from initial research to building, testing, launching, and improving the product.

1. **Problem Discovery & Research:** Understand the problem, target writers, market, writing workflows, existing tools, writer needs, and opportunities in voice-first fiction drafting.
2. **Product Strategy & Definition:** Define the product vision, goals, value proposition, product principles, requirements, MVP scope, business assumptions, and roadmap.
3. **Product Design & User Experience:** Map writer journeys, drafting workflows, recording experiences, transcription review, transformation controls, manuscript organisation, wireframes, prototypes, and interface designs.
4. **Technical Research & System Architecture:** Define technical feasibility, technology stack, frontend and backend architecture, database design, audio processing pipeline, transcription systems, AI transformation workflows, APIs, and integrations.
5. **Engineering & Implementation:** Build the application, implement audio recording and import, transcription, manuscript transformation, editing, project organisation, export, and supporting systems. Manage code and document technical decisions.
6. **Testing, Validation & Quality Assurance:** Test transcription accuracy, transformation fidelity, authorial voice preservation, editing workflows, system reliability, performance, accessibility, and the overall writer experience.
7. **Business Model & Commercial Strategy:** Explore the business model, target customer segments, market positioning, monetisation, pricing, usage economics, and commercial viability.
8. **Go-to-Market, Marketing & Distribution:** Define audience acquisition, product messaging, brand positioning, marketing assets, distribution channels, writer communities, and launch strategy.
9. **Deployment & Release:** Prepare the production environment, configure infrastructure, establish monitoring, manage data security and backups, and coordinate product releases.
10. **Open Source & Developer Documentation:** Prepare the README, installation guide, architecture documentation, API references, licensing, troubleshooting, and contribution guidelines, where applicable.
11. **Results, Evaluation & Iteration:** Evaluate product performance, writer feedback, accepted draft output, correction burden, transcription and transformation quality, engagement, retention, and future improvements.
12. **Project Case Study & Publication:** Document the product journey, demonstrate the application, publish project details, share development progress, and record outcomes and learnings.
